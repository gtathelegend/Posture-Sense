Live Perception Pipeline

How on-device detection works

Your camera feed stays in your browser. Every video frame moves through a 4-step local analysis loop.

/01

Camera Capture

HTML5 MediaDevices API accesses your webcam locally. No video bytes leave your device.

MediaDevices.getUserMedia()
/02

WASM Inference

MediaPipe Tasks Vision WebAssembly extracts 33 3D body keypoints off the main thread in a WebWorker.

poseLandmarker.detect()
/03

Biomechanics

EMA filtering smoothes spatial jitter while joint angles, symmetry, balance, and stability are calculated.

calculateBiomechanics()
/04

Rule & Cues

Measured joint angles are matched against configured rules in yoga_poses.json to output real-time cues.

evaluatePoseRules()
Biomechanical Metrics

What PostureSense measures

Every posture session evaluates 8 core movement dimensions in real time using objective geometric calculations.

🎯

Form Score (0–100%)

Evaluates how accurately your joint positions match the target angle constraints defined in the pose configuration.

⚖️

Biomechanical Symmetry

Compares left and right limb extension, shoulder height levelness, and lateral body alignment.

🧘

Center of Mass Balance

Measures body mass distribution over your support base to detect lean and sway during posture holds.

🛡️

Form Stability

Quantifies spatial steadiness over time, detecting subtle trembling or postural instability.

📐

Range of Motion (ROM)

Calculates exact joint articulation angles (knees, hips, shoulders, spine) during movement transitions.

📷

Tracking Quality

Monitors keypoint visibility confidence so you know when lighting or distance affects camera detection.

⏱️

Sustained Hold Time

Tracks the continuous duration a recognized static pose is held with correct form.

🔢

Repetitions & Tempo

Counts completed exercise reps using state machine transition gates and range-of-motion depth thresholds.

Privacy & Security

100% On-Device Perception

01

Local WebAssembly Execution

PostureSense performs 100% of computer vision pose detection inside your web browser using MediaPipe WebAssembly. Your webcam feed never leaves your device.

  • Zero Video TransmissionNo video frames or raw webcam images are ever uploaded to cloud servers.
  • Zero Raw Landmark PersistenceRaw 33-point keypoint streams are processed transiently in memory and discarded.
  • Telemetry-Only SavingOnly finalized session metrics (scores, duration, rep counts) are saved upon explicit user save.
02

Authenticated User Data Isolation

All persisted session summaries, analytics, and report downloads are protected with strict user isolation guards (IDOR protection).

Your practice history remains strictly private to your authenticated account, protected by encrypted session cookies and strict CORS policies.
