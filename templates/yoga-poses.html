01 — STRENGTH & ALIGNMENT Warrior II

Warrior II Pose

VIRABHADRASANA II

Strengthens legs, arms, and shoulders. Opens hips and chest while developing single-leg stability and core focus.

Configured Rule Constraints

Front Knee Bend: 80° – 110°
Rear Knee Lock: 160° – 180°
Left Shoulder Angle: 80° – 105°
Right Shoulder Angle: 80° – 105°

Execution Steps:

  • Stand with feet wide apart (approx. 3.5 – 4 feet)
  • Turn right foot out 90°, left foot slightly inward
  • Extend arms parallel to the floor in opposite directions
  • Bend front right knee directly over right ankle to 90°
  • Keep torso upright and centered between legs

02 — POSTURE BASELINE T Pose

T Pose

CALIBRATION POSE

Establishes anatomical baseline alignment, evaluates shoulder levelness, and tests bilateral arm extension symmetry.

Configured Rule Constraints

Left Elbow Extension: 165° – 195°
Right Elbow Extension: 165° – 195°
Left Shoulder Angle: 80° – 110°
Right Shoulder Angle: 80° – 110°
Knee Extension: 160° – 195°

Execution Steps:

  • Stand tall with feet hip-width apart and toes pointing forward
  • Extend both arms straight out to the sides horizontally
  • Keep palms facing forward or down with elbows fully locked
  • Maintain a tall, neutral spine with shoulders rolled back
  • Engage core muscles and hold steady for calibration

03 — BALANCE & STABILITY Tree Pose

Tree Pose

VRIKSHASANA

Improves single-leg balance, ankle stability, thigh strength, and mental focus while testing lateral body equilibrium.

Configured Rule Constraints

Standing Leg Knee: 160° – 180°
Lifted Leg Knee: 30° – 90°

Execution Steps:

  • Shift weight onto one standing foot, rooting firmly through the heel
  • Place opposite foot against inner thigh or calf (avoid pressing knee)
  • Bring hands together in prayer position at chest height
  • Keep hips level and squared straight forward
  • Fix gaze on a still point ahead to maintain steady balance

04 — FLEXIBILITY & MOBILITY Cobra Pose

Cobra Pose

BHUJANGASANA

Strengthens the spine extensors, opens chest and lungs, and evaluates thoracic extension flexibility.

Configured Rule Constraints

Spine Extension Arch: 10° – 45°
Left/Right Hip Grounding: 150° – 180°
Knee Extension: 160° – 180°

Execution Steps:

  • Lie prone on stomach with tops of feet flat on floor
  • Place hands beneath shoulders with elbows tucked close
  • Inhale and lift chest off the mat using back muscles
  • Keep hips grounded and shoulders away from ears
  • Maintain a long neck with eyes looking gently forward

Perception Mechanics

How Pose Recognition Works

PostureSense relies on objective, rule-based 3D landmark geometry — not black-box cloud AI or streaming video servers.

/01

Camera Capture

30–60 FPS video input captured via HTML5 MediaDevices API directly in your browser.

/02

WASM Extraction

MediaPipe Tasks Vision WebAssembly extracts 33 3D body keypoints in a dedicated WebWorker.

/03

Landmark Smoothing

Exponential Moving Average (EMA) filtering removes keypoint spatial jitter and recovers missing points.

/04

Joint Geometry

3D vectors calculate exact joint angles (knees, hips, shoulders, spine, elbows) per frame.

/05

Rule Matching

Measured joint angles are matched against bounds defined in yoga_poses.json.

/06

Hold State Machine

Form quality gate triggers continuous hold timer while posture alignment is sustained.
